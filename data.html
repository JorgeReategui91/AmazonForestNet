DATA & OPEN SCIENCE

Data, Protocols and Resources

Open-access protocols, standardized methodologies, metadata, spatial products, and reproducible workflows supporting sustainable forest management and long-term forest monitoring across the Amazon.

OPEN SCIENCE

Sharing data and standardized methodologies

AmazonForestNet promotes open science by providing standardized field protocols, metadata, ecological datasets, spatial products, and analytical workflows that facilitate collaboration, reproducibility, and knowledge exchange among institutions working on tropical forest monitoring and sustainable forest management.

Field Protocols

Standardized protocols for forest inventories, permanent plots, tree measurements, botanical identification, forest monitoring, and sustainable forest management.

Datasets & Metadata

Metadata and ecological datasets from permanent monitoring plots, forest inventories, biodiversity surveys, environmental measurements, and collaborative research projects.

Spatial Products & Tools

LiDAR products, satellite imagery, GIS layers, forest maps, canopy height models, analytical scripts, and reproducible workflows for ecological monitoring.
