Research Themes

Advancing sustainable forest management through long-term monitoring, collaborative research, biodiversity assessment, remote sensing, and knowledge exchange across Amazonian tropical forests.

SCIENTIFIC FRAMEWORK

Advancing sustainable forest management through collaborative science

AmazonForestNet is organized around an integrated scientific framework designed to strengthen sustainable forest management through long-term ecological monitoring, permanent forest inventories, standardized methodologies, and international scientific collaboration across the Amazon Basin.

Our research combines forest ecology, forest inventory, biodiversity assessment, remote sensing, ecological modelling, botanical identification, and capacity building to improve the understanding, monitoring, and sustainable use of tropical forests.

By integrating permanent monitoring plots, forest inventories, LiDAR, satellite products, spatial analysis, and collaborative knowledge exchange between Brazil and Ecuador, AmazonForestNet generates scientific information to support adaptive forest management, biodiversity conservation, climate resilience, and evidence-based environmental policies throughout the Amazon.

THEMES

Core research themes

Nine interconnected themes structure the scientific framework of AmazonForestNet.

1. Sustainable Forest Management

Sustainable forest management is the central pillar of AmazonForestNet. This theme develops science-based approaches to improve timber production while maintaining biodiversity, ecosystem functions, and long-term forest resilience across Amazonian landscapes.

Research integrates forest ecology, silviculture, monitoring, and adaptive management to support evidence-based decision making for tropical forests.

Key questions

  • How can ecological information improve forest management?
  • Which practices maximize long-term sustainability?
  • How can adaptive management be implemented?
  • How can production and conservation be balanced?

2. Long-Term Forest Monitoring

Permanent monitoring plots provide the foundation for understanding forest dynamics through time. This theme focuses on standardized measurements of growth, mortality, recruitment, and forest recovery.

Long-term monitoring generates reliable information for evaluating management practices and ecological resilience across tropical forests.

Key questions

  • How do managed forests change over time?
  • Which variables should be monitored?
  • How do growth and mortality vary among sites?
  • How can monitoring improve forest management?

3. Forest Inventory & Permanent Plots

Reliable forest inventories are essential for sustainable forest management. This theme develops standardized field protocols, permanent plot networks, and inventory methodologies across Amazonian forests.

Special emphasis is placed on harmonizing sampling methods among collaborating institutions.

Key questions

  • How can inventories be standardized?
  • Which sampling methods are most efficient?
  • How can permanent plots support management?
  • How can field data quality be improved?

4. Biodiversity & Botanical Identification

Species identification and biodiversity assessment are fundamental components of long-term ecological monitoring. This theme integrates floristic inventories, taxonomy, and botanical collections.

The objective is to improve species identification and strengthen biodiversity conservation within managed tropical forests.

Key questions

  • How can botanical identification be improved?
  • How does management affect biodiversity?
  • Which species require special conservation attention?
  • How do floristic communities vary across the Amazon?

5. Forest Dynamics & Ecology

Understanding forest dynamics is essential for predicting long-term ecosystem responses. This theme investigates tree growth, mortality, recruitment, regeneration, succession, and ecological interactions.

Results contribute directly to adaptive forest management and sustainable harvesting strategies.

Key questions

  • Which ecological processes regulate forest dynamics?
  • How does forest management affect regeneration?
  • How resilient are managed forests?
  • Which species dominate forest succession?

6. Remote Sensing & Spatial Analysis

Remote sensing technologies complement field inventories by providing regional information on forest structure, biomass, and environmental variability.

This theme integrates LiDAR, satellite imagery, UAVs, GIS, and spatial modelling with permanent forest monitoring.

Key questions

  • How can LiDAR improve forest inventories?
  • How can satellite data support monitoring?
  • Which environmental factors influence forest dynamics?
  • How can spatial analysis improve management?

7. Carbon, Biomass & Climate

Managed tropical forests play an important role in carbon storage and climate regulation. This theme evaluates biomass dynamics, carbon stocks, and ecosystem responses to climate variability.

The research supports sustainable management and contributes to climate mitigation strategies.

Key questions

  • How much carbon is stored in managed forests?
  • How does management influence biomass dynamics?
  • Which forests are most resilient to climate change?
  • How can monitoring support climate policies?

8. Knowledge Exchange & Capacity Building

Knowledge exchange is the foundation of AmazonForestNet. This theme promotes workshops, technical training, protocol standardization, and collaborative learning among institutions throughout the Amazon.

Capacity building strengthens regional expertise in forest monitoring and sustainable management.

Key questions

  • How can technical expertise be shared?
  • How can regional cooperation be strengthened?
  • Which protocols should be standardized?
  • How can training improve forest monitoring?

9. Regional Cooperation & Open Science

AmazonForestNet promotes collaboration among research institutions across the Amazon Basin through shared methodologies, open science, and scientific partnerships.

The network seeks to strengthen international cooperation while improving data accessibility and collaborative research.

Key questions

  • How can institutions collaborate more effectively?
  • How can monitoring networks be integrated?
  • How can ecological data become more accessible?
  • How can international cooperation improve forest management?

SCIENTIFIC VISION

Toward an international reference in sustainable forest management and long-term forest monitoring

Through these integrated research themes, AmazonForestNet seeks to strengthen scientific cooperation across the Amazon Basin by generating knowledge that supports sustainable forest management, biodiversity conservation, long-term ecological monitoring, and evidence-based environmental policies. By integrating permanent forest plots, standardized methodologies, remote sensing, and international collaboration, the network aims to become a regional reference for tropical forest science, capacity building, and knowledge exchange.
