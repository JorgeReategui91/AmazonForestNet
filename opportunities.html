AmazonForestNet welcomes students, researchers, institutions and practitioners interested in advancing knowledge, innovation and sustainable practices for Amazon tropical forest management and monitoring.

MSc and PhD Research

Opportunities for graduate research involving forest dynamics, sustainable management, biodiversity, carbon stocks, remote sensing, ecological monitoring and modeling approaches.

Research opportunities integrating long-term forest monitoring, climate change impacts, forest resilience, data analysis and innovative approaches for tropical forest management.

Scientific Collaboration

Partnerships with researchers, institutions and forest management initiatives interested in data sharing, field campaigns, methodological development and knowledge exchange.
