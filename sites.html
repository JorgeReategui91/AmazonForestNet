MONITORING SITES

AmazonForestNet Monitoring Sites

A collaborative network of permanent forest monitoring sites supporting sustainable forest management, biodiversity conservation, and long-term ecological research across the Amazon.

Ecuadorian Amazon

Permanent monitoring sites established in managed tropical forests of the Ecuadorian Amazon, supporting forest inventories, botanical identification, capacity building, and long-term ecological monitoring through international cooperation.

Brazilian Amazon

Research sites across the Brazilian Amazon integrating permanent plots, sustainable forest management, biodiversity assessment, forest inventory, and long-term ecological monitoring within a collaborative regional network.

Interactive Monitoring Network Map

An interactive map will soon provide access to AmazonForestNet monitoring sites, including permanent plots, managed forests, biodiversity surveys, field expeditions, partner institutions, and collaborative research initiatives across the Amazon Basin.
