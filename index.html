NETWORK FRAMEWORK

Connecting forest monitoring, scientific collaboration, and sustainable forest management across the Amazon

AmazonForestNet is an international collaborative network dedicated to promoting knowledge exchange, sustainable forest management, and long-term ecological monitoring across Amazonian tropical forests. The initiative integrates permanent forest plots, forest inventories, biodiversity monitoring, botanical identification, remote sensing, capacity building, and open science to strengthen regional cooperation and support evidence-based forest management.
