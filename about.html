About AmazonForestNet

An international collaborative network promoting knowledge exchange, sustainable forest management, and long-term forest monitoring across Amazonian tropical forests.

WHO WE ARE

An international network for sustainable forest management and long-term forest monitoring

AmazonForestNet is an international collaborative initiative dedicated to strengthening sustainable forest management through long-term ecological monitoring, permanent forest inventories, scientific cooperation, and capacity building across the Amazon Basin. The network promotes standardized field protocols, knowledge exchange, and collaborative research to improve the understanding, conservation, and sustainable use of tropical forests.

The initiative was established to connect researchers, institutions, forest managers, and students working throughout the Amazon, fostering regional cooperation and supporting evidence-based forest management. AmazonForestNet integrates permanent plots, biodiversity monitoring, forest inventories, botanical identification, remote sensing, and data sharing to advance tropical forest science and sustainable management.

OUR MISSION

Mission

To strengthen sustainable forest management across the Amazon through knowledge exchange, long-term ecological monitoring, standardized forest inventories, scientific collaboration, and capacity building among research institutions, forest managers, and local communities.

Vision

To become a leading collaborative network supporting science-based forest management and long-term monitoring throughout the Amazon Basin, promoting regional cooperation, open science, biodiversity conservation, and resilient forest landscapes for future generations.

People Behind AmazonForestNet

AmazonForestNet brings together researchers from Brazil and Ecuador with expertise in sustainable forest management, long-term forest monitoring, forest inventories, biodiversity conservation, ecological modelling, remote sensing, and capacity building to strengthen collaborative research across the Amazon.
