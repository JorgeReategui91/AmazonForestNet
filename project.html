PROAMAZON

Knowledge Exchange for Tree Monitoring in the Management of Amazon Tropical Forests

ProAmazon is an international cooperation initiative coordinated by AmazonForestNet to strengthen sustainable forest management through knowledge exchange, technical training, and long-term ecological monitoring across Amazonian tropical forests.

The project promotes collaboration between research institutions in Brazil and Ecuador by standardizing forest monitoring methodologies, establishing permanent plots, improving botanical identification, integrating forest inventories with remote sensing technologies, and building technical capacity for adaptive forest management.

PROJECT COMPONENTS

Scientific and technical activities

Forest Inventory

Standardized forest inventories, permanent plots, tree measurements, botanical identification, and long-term monitoring protocols.

Long-Term Monitoring

Monitoring forest dynamics, tree growth, mortality, recruitment, regeneration, and ecological responses to forest management.

Capacity Building

Technical training, workshops, knowledge exchange, and protocol standardization for researchers and forest technicians.

Remote Sensing

LiDAR, satellite imagery, GIS, canopy height models, and spatial analysis supporting forest monitoring.

Forest Ecology

Forest dynamics, biodiversity, species composition, ecological processes, and sustainable management.

Regional Cooperation

Scientific collaboration between Brazil and Ecuador, institutional networking, open science, and data sharing across Amazonian forests.
